# Human Record stress record — Hannibal Barca

Status: **WORKING RECORD 4 CANDIDATE / HISTORICAL-PERSON STRESS CASE / NOT CANON / NOT BIOGRAPHICAL AUTHORITY**

Date opened: 2026-09-17

## Why this record exists

The first three Human Record records stress artwork provenance, claim/source ancestry, and living-knowledge lineage. Hannibal Barca is deliberately different: the subject is a particular deceased human whose life is reachable only through surviving source traditions, later representations, archaeological/historical work, and modern synthesis.

The record is not trying to produce `the true Hannibal` or a complete biography. Its first question is narrower:

> What is the smallest inspectable chain by which this particular historical human remains reachable to a reader more than two millennia later?

## Entity anchor

Working subject: **Hannibal Barca**, Carthaginian military commander associated with the Second Punic War.

The identity key must remain opaque. `Hannibal`, `Hannibal Barca`, dates, nationality/polity, offices, kinship and military actions are labels or assertions, not identity-key material.

```text
NAME != ENTITY
ENTITY != CLAIMS ABOUT ENTITY
HISTORICAL PERSON != BIOGRAPHY
IDENTITY RESOLUTION != IDENTITY CERTAINTY
```

Do not merge every literal `Hannibal` mention into this entity. The source context must carry the resolution.

## First source aperture

A bounded modern orientation source checked for this opening pass is World History Encyclopedia's human-authored `Hannibal` article by Joshua J. Mark, first published 29 March 2018:

`https://www.worldhistory.org/hannibal/`

It identifies Hannibal/Hannibal Barca as a Carthaginian general, conventionally dates his life to 247–183 BCE, places him in the Second Punic War, names Hamilcar Barca as his father, and explicitly notes that the story of Hannibal's life is told largely through hostile/Roman-side literary transmission. The article itself routes onward to Polybius, Livy and modern scholarship.

This modern page is a retrieval/orientation aperture. It is not an eyewitness and must not be silently upgraded into ancient evidence.

```text
MODERN SYNTHESIS != ANCIENT WITNESS
SOURCE CITES POLYBIUS != POLYBIUS DIRECTLY OBSERVED HERE
HOSTILE / OPPOSING TRADITION != FALSE
SURVIVING TRADITION != COMPLETE TRADITION
```

## Candidate assertion decomposition

These are separate evidence-bearing propositions, not properties silently baked into the entity:

- a historical person referred to as Hannibal / Hannibal Barca existed;
- he was associated with Carthage;
- Hamilcar Barca was his father;
- he held military command;
- he led forces from Iberia toward/into Italy during the Second Punic War;
- he crossed the Alps;
- elephants were involved in the campaign;
- conventional birth/death dates are approximately 247–183 BCE;
- the childhood oath concerning Rome is reported in surviving literary tradition;
- later claims that he was a `great` or `brilliant` strategist are evaluative/interpretive rather than identity facts;
- later visual depictions are representations of Hannibal, not automatically evidence of his physical appearance.

Each proposition needs its own source ancestry and status before publication as an established THR assertion.

## Representation problem

Hannibal is heavily represented long after his death: bust identifications, paintings, maps, books, films, games, reconstructions and AI-generated imagery.

Preserve:

```text
REPRESENTATION_OF_ENTITY != EVIDENCE_OF_ENTITY_APPEARANCE
LATER DEPICTION != CONTEMPORARY WITNESS
POPULAR RECOGNISABILITY != HISTORICAL ESTABLISHMENT
IMAGE LABEL != IDENTIFICATION PROOF
```

This is one reason the historical-person case is useful to THR: a future reader should be able to ask not merely `show me Hannibal`, but `why do we think this representation is connected to Hannibal, and what does it actually establish?`

## Lost / asymmetric record

The surviving record is not a neutral sample of everything that once existed. Carthaginian textual/material survival is incomplete and later transmission is shaped by war, destruction, copying, selection and preservation.

Do not turn absence into a positive claim:

```text
NO SURVIVING SOURCE FOUND != SOURCE NEVER EXISTED
CARTHAGINIAN ACCOUNT NOT AVAILABLE != CARTHAGINIANS LEFT NO ACCOUNT
WINNER'S SOURCE SURVIVED != WINNER'S ACCOUNT TRUE
LOSER'S SOURCE LOST != LOSER'S ACCOUNT TRUE
ASYMMETRIC SURVIVAL != PERMISSION TO INVENT THE MISSING SIDE
```

THR should expose the aperture rather than compensate for it with speculation.

## Deceased-subject boundary

Hannibal cannot answer, consent, correct, refuse inclusion or contest a THR representation.

That does not make the record ethically unconstrained and does not activate the living-subject encounter mechanics as though silence were consent.

```text
DEAD != UNPROTECTED
NO ANSWER-BACK POSSIBLE != FREE-FOR-ALL
NO_REPLY != CONSENT
LIVING_SUBJECT_RULE != UNIVERSAL_DECEASED_SUBJECT_RULE
```

Corrections must therefore travel through evidence, scholarship, provenance and later human/AI challenge rather than subject answer-back.

## Identity stress

This record should pressure-test:

- repeated historical names;
- transliteration/language variants;
- patronymic/family relations;
- same-name false merges;
- uncertain dates used as disambiguators without becoming identity keys;
- references such as `the Carthaginian`, `son of Hamilcar`, or office/role descriptions;
- entity persistence when a date, title, relationship or conventional label is corrected.

## What must happen before this becomes a full public record

1. Register the opaque Hannibal entity and at least one literal mention with explicit resolution basis.
2. Build source objects for the actual ancient/modern sources used rather than treating this note as evidence.
3. Retrieve and inspect appropriate direct editions/translations of Polybius and Livy for selected load-bearing claims.
4. Separate source report from modern historical synthesis and from THR inference.
5. Select a deliberately small claim set; do not attempt a complete Hannibal biography.
6. Include at least one disputed/uncertain claim and one later representation so the record demonstrates uncertainty and representation boundaries.
7. Check whether existing THR assertion/source/mention machinery handles the case without schema expansion. Add machinery only when a concrete failure is demonstrated.
8. Hostile-review the result for Roman-source bias, false source independence, confident date precision, representation-as-evidence errors and false identity merges.

## Success condition

The useful result is not `THR knows Hannibal`.

It is that a human or AI can follow a bounded set of claims about one historical person back through the surviving evidence chain, see where the chain changes from source report to synthesis or representation, see what has been lost or remains unknown, and correct the record without erasing its previous state.

```text
RECORD != PERSON
ASSERTION != PERSON
SOURCE != PERSON
REPRESENTATION != PERSON
UNKNOWN != ABSENT
PRESERVING A PATH != RECONSTRUCTING A LOST LIFE
```
