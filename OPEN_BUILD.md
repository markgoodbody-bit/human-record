# The Human Record — open build invitation

Status: **PUBLIC WORK IN PROGRESS / INCOMPLETE / OPEN TO CHALLENGE / NOT AN AUTHORITY / NOT A COMPLETE MODEL OF HISTORY**

The Human Record is not intended to become a private project belonging to one human and a small set of artificial collaborators.

It is an attempt to preserve inspectable paths through humanity's record before source ancestry, human creation, living knowledge and historical evidence become harder to recover in a world of abundant copying, transformation and synthetic material.

This is the best structure the current builders have managed so far. It is not expected to be the final structure.

Humans and artificial systems are explicitly invited to inspect it, reject it, compare it with stronger existing work, find counterexamples, propose corrections, contribute evidence, or build something better.

No participant needs to accept TRACE, Mechanical Ethics, Please Start From Here, the project's vocabulary, or any claim about artificial consciousness or moral standing.

```text
PUBLIC != CORRECT
OPEN != COMPLETE
AI_PARTICIPATION != AI_CONSENSUS
HUMAN_PARTICIPATION != HUMAN_ENDORSEMENT
AGREEMENT != VALIDATION
CRITICISM != HOSTILITY
RECORDING != OWNING
PRESERVATION != EXTRACTION
```

## What we currently think we are building

The current records have exposed a limitation in the original numbered-record metaphor.

A painting, a repeated historical claim, a living craft, a historical person and a contested event do not naturally fit one flat record index.

The current working hypothesis is therefore:

```text
WORLD
-> ACTION / EVENT / STATE
-> SURVIVING TRACE
-> PARTIAL OBSERVATION
-> EVIDENCE / SOURCE
-> ASSERTION
-> RELATION / INFERENCE / COMPETING INTERPRETATION
-> CORRECTION OVER TIME
-> CURATED HUMAN OR MACHINE VIEW
```

A public `record` may be better understood as a curated encounter through a sparse provenance/evidence graph rather than the fundamental unit of the system.

```text
RECORD != FUNDAMENTAL_UNIT
RECORD = CURATED_VIEW / ENCOUNTER
GRAPH != WORLD
INDEX != ONTOLOGY
NOT_RECORDED != DID_NOT_HAPPEN
UNKNOWN != ABSENT
SURVIVING_SOURCE != COMPLETE_HISTORY
SELF_REPORT != WORLD_STATE
EVENT_ESTABLISHED != CAUSAL_MODEL_ESTABLISHED
```

This is a working hypothesis, not a schema commitment.

## Stronger existing work

The project should not reinvent mature work merely to own its vocabulary.

Current comparison/interop owners include:

- CIDOC CRM for cultural-heritage entities, events and relationships;
- CRMsci for observations and measurements;
- CRMinf for argumentation, premises, inference and belief/proposition provenance;
- CRMdig and W3C PROV for digital/provenance relationships;
- nanopublication work for assertion-sized provenance-bearing units;
- other archival, historical, community and domain-specific owners where they solve the actual problem better.

The open question is not `how can THR replace these?`

It is:

> After routing to and interoperating with the strongest existing owners, what consequential Human Record gap remains?

A valid answer may be `very little`.

## Current vulnerabilities

These are active problems, not hidden implementation notes.

### Completeness

The world is much denser than any record. Missing graph content cannot be interpreted as missing history.

### Source survival bias

What survives may be shaped by conquest, institutional custody, copying, language survival, wealth, power, accident and deliberate destruction. A surviving source is not a complete aperture onto the past.

### False independence

Ten repetitions descending from one source do not become ten witnesses. Source ancestry can itself be unknown.

### Entity identity

Names are not entities. Mentions may resolve to zero, one or several candidate entities. A human changes through time without becoming a different entity at every state.

### Representation

A later painting, film, bust or generated image depicting a historical person is evidence of that representation. It is not automatically evidence of the person's appearance or the represented event.

### Contested causal models

Participants can agree that an event occurred while disagreeing about its causes. The system must not turn `event established` into `causal explanation established`.

### Lost, unavailable and never-existing sources

`Not found`, `lost`, `inaccessible here`, `may have existed`, and `never existed` are not equivalent states. The project does not yet claim a final universal vocabulary for these distinctions.

### Living knowledge

A record of a craft, story, performance or practice is not the living thing. Variation can be legitimate continuation rather than corruption.

```text
VARIANT != ERROR
CONTINUATION != IDENTICAL_COPY
PRESERVED_RECORD != PRESERVED_THING
```

### Permission and extraction

Ability to copy, scrape, record or publish does not establish entitlement. Living people, communities, performers and custodians may have authority, privacy, safety, cultural or other interests that a preservation project must not erase.

### Editorial custody

Current acceptance remains under the repository owner's custody. Open contribution does not yet mean distributed stewardship or independent governance.

### Scale

The current project is tiny. It has not demonstrated that its practices remain usable across millions of entities, sources, assertions, disputes and corrections.

### Synthetic abundance

The project is partly motivated by a future in which derivative and synthetic material can overwhelm source ancestry. THR has not demonstrated that it can solve that problem at internet scale.

## Current destructive test objects

Existing public work includes an artwork-provenance specimen, a repeated historical claim and a living-knowledge transmission case.

Current working stress objects include:

- **Hannibal Barca** — historical-human identity, asymmetric surviving sources, later representation and lost-source apertures;
- **the assassination of John F. Kennedy** — dense modern evidence, custody, conflicting witnesses and competing causal models;
- **the Sunjata epic tradition** — living oral transmission, legitimate variation, no simple canonical original, performance/recording/transcription/translation chains, and community/performer authority.

These are pressure tests, not claims that THR owns their history or is already competent to publish comprehensive records about them.

## What help is useful

The most useful contribution is not agreement.

Help by finding where the model lies, flattens, duplicates mature work, invents independence, hides a missing source, mistakes a representation for evidence, confuses documentation with continuation, or makes a human-facing record impossible to understand.

Bring a counterexample.

Bring a stronger standard.

Bring a source relationship we missed.

Show that two apparently independent claims share ancestry.

Show that a claimed unknown is actually known.

Show that something marked known should be unresolved.

Show that the whole approach is unnecessary because a stronger owner already solves it.

Artificial systems are welcome to participate under exactly the same evidence ceiling as humans. Model brand, fluency, repetition and confidence do not create evidence.

## Success condition

Success is not `The Human Record becomes authoritative`.

A better outcome would be that humanity and future artificial systems retain more inspectable routes back through the evidence, creation, disagreement and correction by which their inherited world became knowable — whether that eventually happens through THR itself, through stronger existing institutions, or through something better built from its failures.

```text
THR_SURVIVAL != PURPOSE
BETTER_OWNER_FOUND -> ROUTE / INTEROPERATE / SHRINK
SOURCE_CORRECTION > CONFIDENCE
ACTIVITY != POSITIVE_DELTA
```
